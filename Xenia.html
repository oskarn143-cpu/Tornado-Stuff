Xenia F5 Tornado - April 3, 1974

Detailed information about the deadly Xenia F5 Tornado that struck Ohio.

Overview

The Xenia Tornado was part of the 1974 Super Outbreak, one of the most violent tornado outbreaks in U.S. history. It devastated the city of Xenia, Ohio.

  • Date: April 3, 1974
  • Location: Xenia, Ohio
  • Rating: F5
  • Path Length: 40 miles
  • Max Width: 0.5 miles
  • Fatalities: 34
  • Injuries: Over 1,000

Damage and Impact

The tornado destroyed much of Xenia, including homes, businesses, and infrastructure. It was one of 148 tornados in the outbreak that killed 319 people.

Weather Conditions

Occurred during a massive outbreak with extreme atmospheric conditions across the Midwest and South.

Lessons Learned

Led to advancements in tornado forecasting and public awareness campaigns.
