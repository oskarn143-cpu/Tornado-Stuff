Joplin EF5 Tornado - May 22, 2011

Detailed information about the catastrophic Joplin EF5 Tornado that struck Missouri.

Overview

The Joplin Tornado was one of the deadliest in U.S. history, striking the city of Joplin, Missouri, during the evening rush hour.

  • Date: May 22, 2011
  • Location: Joplin, Missouri
  • Rating: EF5
  • Path Length: 22.1 miles
  • Max Width: 1 mile
  • Fatalities: 158
  • Injuries: Over 1,000

Damage and Impact

The tornado destroyed a third of Joplin, including St. John's Regional Medical Center. It caused $2.8 billion in damages and led to massive rebuilding efforts.

Weather Conditions

Formed from a supercell with high instability and wind shear, part of a larger outbreak.

Lessons Learned

Improved emergency response and the use of social media for warnings.
