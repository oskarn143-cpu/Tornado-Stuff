Jarrell F5 Tornado - May 27, 1997

Detailed information about the Jarrell F5 Tornado that devastated central Texas.

Overview

The Jarrell Tornado was part of a series of tornados that struck Texas on May 27, 1997, known as the Central Texas Tornado.

  • Date: May 27, 1997
  • Location: Jarrell, Texas
  • Rating: F5
  • Path Length: 7.6 miles
  • Max Width: 0.5 miles
  • Fatalities: 27
  • Injuries: Over 100

Damage and Impact

The tornado destroyed much of Jarrell, including homes and a school. It was one of three F5 tornados that day.

Weather Conditions

Formed in a highly unstable environment with strong thunderstorms.

Lessons Learned

Highlighted the vulnerability of rural communities and the need for better shelters.
