Enderlin EF5 Tornado - June 15, 2025

Detailed information about the devastating Enderlin EF5 Tornado that struck North Dakota in 2025.

Overview

The Enderlin EF5 Tornado was one of the most powerful tornados recorded in recent history. It touched down on June 15, 2025, near Enderlin, North Dakota, and traveled for over 50 miles, causing catastrophic damage.

  • Date: June 15, 2025
  • Location: Enderlin, North Dakota
  • Rating: EF5
  • Path Length: 52 miles
  • Max Width: 1.5 miles
  • Fatalities: 47
  • Injuries: Over 300

Damage and Impact

The tornado obliterated entire neighborhoods, with winds estimated at over 200 mph. Homes were reduced to rubble, and infrastructure was severely damaged. The economic cost was in the billions.

Rescue efforts lasted for weeks, with national aid pouring in to help rebuild the affected areas.

Weather Conditions

The tornado formed from a supercell thunderstorm with extreme instability. High CAPE values and strong wind shear contributed to its intensity.

Lessons Learned

This event highlighted the importance of advanced warning systems and community preparedness. It led to improved tornado detection technology in the region.
