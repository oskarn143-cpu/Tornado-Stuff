Hackleburg EF5 Tornado - April 27, 2011

Detailed information about the powerful Hackleburg EF5 Tornado that devastated parts of Alabama.

Overview

The Hackleburg Tornado was part of the April 27, 2011 tornado outbreak. It was one of the widest EF5 tornados on record, with a path of destruction over 80 miles long.

  • Date: April 27, 2011
  • Location: Hackleburg, Alabama
  • Rating: EF5
  • Path Length: 80.7 miles
  • Max Width: 1.75 miles
  • Fatalities: 72
  • Injuries: Over 200

Damage and Impact

The tornado caused catastrophic damage, leveling entire communities. It was noted for its unusual width and the way it carved a path through rural areas, leaving little standing in its wake.

Weather Conditions

Formed from a supercell with high helicity and instability, part of a larger outbreak that produced multiple violent tornados.

Lessons Learned

Emphasized the need for better rural warning systems and rapid response teams.
