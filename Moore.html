Moore EF5 Tornado - May 20, 2013

Detailed information about the Moore EF5 Tornado that struck Oklahoma.

Overview

The Moore Tornado was one of the widest EF5 tornados, devastating the city of Moore, Oklahoma, and surrounding areas.

  • Date: May 20, 2013
  • Location: Moore, Oklahoma
  • Rating: EF5
  • Path Length: 17 miles
  • Max Width: 1.3 miles
  • Fatalities: 24
  • Injuries: Over 200

Damage and Impact

The tornado destroyed two schools and thousands of homes. It was the second EF5 to strike Moore, Oklahoma.

Weather Conditions

Formed from a supercell with extreme instability and wind shear.

Lessons Learned

Led to stricter building codes and improved school safety protocols.
